Every contour glyph is deterministically generated by a pure function contourGlyph(seed, size) using a 32-bit FNV-1a hash of the record ID and a mulberry32 PRNG (±18% radial perturbation), smoothed into 4–6 closed Catmull-Rom spline rings.
